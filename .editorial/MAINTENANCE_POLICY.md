# Maintenance Policy

## Review Cadence

### Every contribution
- technical review;
- teaching review;
- source review;
- version review where relevant;
- licensing/originality review.

### Monthly
- broken-link check;
- unresolved editorial issues;
- lab verification status;
- stale TODO/UNVERIFIED content.

### At every Phase boundary
- job-market refresh;
- curriculum weight review;
- canonical resource review;
- hardware/toolchain baseline review.

### Quarterly
Perform a deeper curriculum review:

- what produced measurable learning;
- what repeatedly caused confusion;
- what skills are appearing more/less often in target roles;
- what resources have improved or become stale;
- which chapters should be rewritten, merged, downgraded, or removed.

### After flagship projects
Write a project postmortem and update the curriculum based on actual failure modes.

## Staleness Signals

Trigger review when:

- a project deprecates or changes an API;
- kernel/bootloader/RTOS behavior materially changes;
- a canonical upstream resource becomes abandoned;
- a board loses useful upstream support;
- repeated learner failures reveal a prerequisite gap;
- job-market evidence shifts skill priorities.

## Deletion Is Allowed

The repository optimizes learning return, not content volume.

A chapter may be:

- rewritten;
- merged;
- moved to optional;
- archived;
- deleted.

Historical decisions should be recorded when the change is significant.

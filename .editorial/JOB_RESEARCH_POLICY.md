# Job Market Research Policy

## Purpose

Career research exists to keep curriculum weight aligned with valuable real-world work without turning the curriculum into short-lived keyword training.

## Target Role Families

Track at minimum:

- Embedded Linux;
- BSP;
- Linux Driver;
- Linux Kernel;
- Firmware / RTOS;
- SoC / Platform Software;
- performance engineering;
- robotics/edge-AI system software as adjacent paths.

## Geography

Research should include:

- Mainland China;
- international roles where technically comparable.

## Sampling

Prefer a diverse employer sample:

- semiconductor companies;
- device/product companies;
- automotive/robotics companies;
- infrastructure/platform companies;
- major international engineering organizations.

## Frequency

- refresh at each major Phase boundary;
- perform a deeper quarterly review;
- perform an immediate review if a major technology/market shift affects the curriculum.

## Output

Job research should produce:

- repeated skill requirements;
- high-value differentiators;
- role-specific skills;
- experience-level expectations;
- tooling trends;
- skills that appear to be losing relevance;
- mapping to existing curriculum modules;
- proposed additions/deletions/downgrades.

Do not add a topic merely because one employer mentions it.
